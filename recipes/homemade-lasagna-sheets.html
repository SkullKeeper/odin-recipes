Homemade Lasagna Sheet

Description

These homemade lasagna noodles don't need to be cooked before going in the oven.

If you don't have a pasta machine, you can make this recipe using a rolling pin.

Ingredients

  • 2 ¼ cups Italian-style tipo 00 flour, plus additional for dusting
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 tablespoon water as needed
