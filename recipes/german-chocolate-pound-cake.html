German Chocolate Pound Cake

Description

This German chocolate cake tastes like a rich, buttery chocolate pound cake, so chocolatey and moist that it may remind you of a brownie. Topped with just the right amount of sweet coconut-pecan frosting, it delivers the rich flavors of classic German chocolate cake without all the layers and extra work.

Ingredients For Cake

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon instant espresso coffee powder
  • 1 1/2 cups butter, softened
  • 2 1/2 cups white sugar
  • 5 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 1 1/2 cups buttermilk, at room temperature
  • 2 ounces sweet baking chocolate, such as Baker's® German's, melted and cooled
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract

Ingredients For Coconut-Pecan Frosting

  • 1 (5-ounce) can evaporated milk
  • 3/4 cup white sugar
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 2 large egg yolks
  • 1 1/3 cups flaked coconut
  • 1/2 cup chopped toasted pecans

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 325 degrees F (165 degrees C). Generously grease a 10-inch fluted tube nonstick pan (such as Bundt®) with nonstick spray for baking.
  2. Whisk together flour, cocoa powder, salt, baking powder, and espresso powder in a medium bowl.
  3. Beat butter with an electric mixer in a large bowl for 30 seconds. Add sugar and beat until light and fluffy, about 5 minutes. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition. Beat in flour mixture in two additions.
  4. Beat in buttermilk, melted chocolate, and vanilla until combined. Spread batter in prepared pan.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean, 65 to 70 minutes. Cool in pan on a wire rack for 10 minutes. Invert onto the wire rack and let cool completely, about 2 hours.
  6. For Coconut-Pecan Frosting, combine evaporated milk, sugar, butter, and egg yolks in a small saucepan over medium heat. Cook and stir until thickened and the mixture coats the back of a metal spoon, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat. Stir in coconut and pecans. Cover and let cool before spooning over cooled cake.
